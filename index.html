Functional impact guide

Green (Go)
Mild impact. You have a headache, but you can still function and keep up with normal daily tasks.
Yellow (Slow Down)
Moderate impact. Functioning is slowed or takes extra effort, and you may need to pace yourself or scale back plans.
Red (Stop)
Severe impact. You need to stop what you are doing, rest, or lie down, and you may miss work, school, or caregiving duties.

Symptom-free: No headache or migraine.
Unlogged: No entry recorded; not counted as symptom-free.

Install & backup

Install using your browser menu (Install app or Add to Home Screen). On iOS, use Safari’s Share menu. Once loaded online, the app can work offline.

Entries stay in this browser, unencrypted. Clearing site data or losing your device may erase them. Export backups regularly. Reports and backups contain private health information.
